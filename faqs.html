Frequently Asked Questions.

Helpful answers about print, artwork, quotations, production, delivery and working with our team.

Need something else? Contact us.

1. What can EDENDAY print and produce?

We work across digital and offset printing, stationery, books and documents, packaging, business and marketing materials, promotional gifts, invitations, banners and signage. Visit the categories page to browse products and formats.

2. How do I request a quote?

Send your brief through the Contact page, email info@edenday.ae, or call +971 56 825 8470. Include the product, approximate quantity, size, material or finish, artwork status, required date and delivery location when known.

3. Can you help prepare or check my artwork?

Yes. Tell us whether your artwork is ready or you need assistance. We can discuss file requirements, layout checks and design support for your project before production.

4. Will I see a proof before production?

Where the project requires it, we will share a proof or production details for your review. Please check names, spelling, dimensions, colours and other supplied information carefully. Production timing begins after the required approval and order details are confirmed.

5. Can I customize a product?

Many products can be tailored by size, paper, material, print method, finish, colour, quantity and branding. Available options depend on the product. Share your preferences in your brief and we will confirm what can be made.

6. Is there a minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities vary by product and production method. Tell us the quantity you have in mind; we can advise on suitable options and how quantity affects the quote.

7. How long will production and delivery take?

Timing depends on the product, quantity, artwork readiness, approvals and production schedule. We will confirm an estimated timeline with your quotation or order confirmation and coordinate delivery across the UAE where available.

8. What payment options are available?

Payment terms and any required deposit are confirmed in the written quotation or order confirmation. Please use the payment instructions provided for your specific order.

9. Can EDENDAY help with an event?

Yes. Our event coordination service can help plan print, branded materials and event details together. Visit the Event Coordination page or contact us with your event date, location and requirements.

10. Where can I find the catalogues?

Current catalogue links are available in the Our catalogues page on the home page. Catalogue products and availability may change; contact us to confirm details for a specific item.

11. How do I follow up on an existing enquiry?

Reply to the email conversation or contact us by phone or email and include your name, company and any quotation or order reference you have. This helps our team locate the right project.

12. How can I reach the team?

Call +971 56 825 8470, email info@edenday.ae, or use the contact options on our Contact page. EDENDAY is based in Ajman, United Arab Emirates.
