Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how EDENDAY BRANDING & ADVERTISING handles personal information when you visit our website or contact our team.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your information

EDENDAY BRANDING & ADVERTISING, based in Ajman, United Arab Emirates, is responsible for personal information it receives directly through the contact channels listed on this website. For privacy questions, contact info@edenday.ae or call +971 56 825 8470.

2. Information you may share

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, you may choose to share your name, email address, phone number, company name, project brief, delivery details, artwork, preferences and correspondence. We ask that you share only information relevant to your enquiry or order.

Do not send payment-card details, passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information through general email or messaging unless we have specifically requested it through a suitable secure channel.

3. Information handled by this website

Your browser and the site’s hosting infrastructure may process standard technical information needed to deliver and secure web pages, such as an IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps and error information. Hosting providers may maintain access or security logs as part of operating their systems.

The on-page project enquiry forms currently validate fields and show a confirmation in your browser; they do not transmit form entries to an EDENDAY server. The newsletter form likewise does not submit or store the email address. For an actual project enquiry, please contact us using the email address, phone number or WhatsApp link shown on the site.

4. How we use information received directly

Information you send us through direct contact channels may be used to:

  • reply to your question and discuss a requested quote;
  • confirm specifications, artwork, proofs, quantities, production and delivery;
  • provide customer support and keep relevant business records;
  • protect our communications, identify misuse and address legal obligations or claims.

We do not sell personal information or use enquiry details for unrelated marketing. If we introduce a newsletter or promotional messages in the future, we will provide an appropriate way to choose whether to receive them.

5. Legal grounds and choices

Where applicable, we handle personal information on a basis permitted by UAE law, such as responding to a request you make, performing or preparing for an agreed service, meeting a legal obligation, protecting rights, or using your consent where consent is required.

6. Sharing information

We may share relevant information with authorised team members and service providers when needed to respond to you or deliver a requested service, such as providers supporting communications, website hosting, production or delivery. We may also disclose information where required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

We limit sharing to information relevant to the task. We do not sell or rent personal information to advertisers.

7. External websites, fonts and international processing

This website links to third-party sites, including external product catalogues and WhatsApp. It also loads fonts hosted by Google Fonts. When your browser opens an external service or requests a hosted resource, that provider may receive technical connection data directly from your device. The provider’s own privacy notice applies to that processing.

If a service provider processes personal information for us outside the UAE, any transfer will be handled in accordance with applicable UAE data-protection requirements.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently provide user accounts or online checkout. We do not knowingly use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies on the site. Your browser or an external service may use technical storage needed to operate its own service; you can review browser settings and the external provider’s privacy information for more detail.

9. Retention and security

We keep information received directly only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, provide or support the requested service, maintain appropriate business records and meet legal requirements. Retention periods can vary depending on the nature of the project and any applicable record-keeping obligations.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information we hold from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your data-protection requests

Subject to applicable law and its conditions, you may ask us about personal information we hold about you, request correction or completion of inaccurate information, request erasure where available, or ask us to restrict or stop processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, without affecting processing already carried out lawfully.

Email info@edenday.ae with “Privacy request” in the subject line and explain what you are asking us to do. We may request information to verify your identity and will respond in accordance with applicable requirements. Some requests may be limited where the law requires or permits us to retain or use information.

11. Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults making print or branding enquiries. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information directly, please contact us so we can review the matter.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this page if our website or information-handling practices change. The latest version and its effective date will be posted here. For general information about UAE personal-data protections, see the UAE Government data-protection guidance and the official Personal Data Protection Law.
