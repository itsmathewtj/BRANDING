GOOD QUESTIONS, GOOD ANSWERS

Frequently asked
questions.

Still curious? Talk to a real person ->

What print and packaging services do you offer?+

We produce business stationery, marketing materials, books and documents, custom packaging, labels, invitations, promotional items and large-format signage. Share your brief and we’ll help identify the right format and finish.

What information do you need to prepare a quote?+

Include the product, quantity, dimensions, preferred materials or finishes, delivery location and target date. If you’re still deciding, tell us what the print needs to do and we can recommend suitable options.

How long does production and delivery take?+

Timing varies with the product, quantity, finishing and delivery destination. Many standard orders take around 3–7 business days after artwork approval. We’ll confirm the schedule for your specific order before production begins.

Do you deliver to all Emirates in the UAE?+

Yes. We arrange delivery across all seven Emirates. Share your destination and preferred timeline so we can confirm the available delivery option and include it in your quote.

What artwork files do you need?+

A print-ready PDF is usually best. File setup can vary by product, so we’ll share the relevant size, bleed, colour and resolution requirements for your job. If you need help preparing artwork, ask our team about design support.

Can I review a proof before printing?+

Yes. You can review and approve the artwork proof before production starts. Please check names, spelling, colours, quantities and other supplied details carefully when reviewing it.

What is the minimum order quantity?+

Minimum quantities depend on the product and production method. Many items can start from 50 pieces. Tell us what you’re planning and we’ll confirm the available quantities and pricing.

Can you help with design and finishing choices?+

We can guide you through paper stocks, formats and finishing options to suit your brief. If you need artwork created or adapted, contact us to discuss the design support available for your project.
